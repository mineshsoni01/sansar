# Product Process

This document describes the end-to-end product process used across the SANSAR platform.

## Flow

0. **Process** – the operating model and governance that PM, Product Design, and Engineering follow, owned per department (`00-process/`).
1. **Direction** – vision, strategy, roadmap, and objectives set the direction for the product (`01-direction/`).
2. **Discovery** – problems and opportunities are researched and validated (`02-discovery/`).
3. **Journeys** – customer journeys are mapped to understand the end-to-end experience (`03-journeys/`).
4. **PRD** – validated opportunities are turned into product requirement documents (`04-prd/`).
5. **UI/UX** – requirements are translated into information architecture and interface designs (`05-ui/`).
6. **Analytics** – shipped features are measured for adoption and behaviour (`06-analytics/`).
7. **Decisions** – significant product and architecture decisions are recorded for traceability (`07-decisions/`).

Templates and conventions used across these stages live in `08-standards/`. Retired material moves to `09-archive/`.

Folder numbers reflect this same order — see [08-standards/02-naming/README.md](08-standards/02-naming/README.md).
