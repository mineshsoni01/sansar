# SANSAR Product Docs

SANSAR is a unified physical security platform covering video surveillance, access control, and visitor management.

This repository is the single source of truth for product direction, discovery, customer journeys, PRDs, UI/UX documentation, analytics, and decisions.

## Structure

Folders are numbered so they sort in the same order as the product process flow. See
[08-standards/02-naming/README.md](08-standards/02-naming/README.md) for the full naming convention.

- `00-process/` – PM/PD/Engineering operating process, organized by department (owner)
- `01-direction/` – vision, strategy, roadmap, objectives, principles
- `02-discovery/` – research and discovery work, organized by year/quarter
- `03-journeys/` – customer journey maps, organized by year/quarter
- `04-prd/` – product requirement documents, organized by year/quarter
- `05-ui/` – information architecture and UI/UX documentation per product area
- `06-analytics/` – product analytics, adoption, behaviour, experiments
- `07-decisions/` – product and architecture decision records
- `08-standards/` – templates, naming, glossary, writing guidelines, review checklists
- `09-archive/` – retired and legacy documentation

See [PRODUCT_PROCESS.md](PRODUCT_PROCESS.md) for how these pieces fit together, and [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for how to contribute.
